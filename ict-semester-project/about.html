About EcoGlow Furnishings

We are a student-run concept brand dedicated to merging eco-friendly materials with smart home experiences. Our Fall 2025 objective is to prototype a fully functional website that highlights our business model and engages users through educational content.

Vision

Make sustainable interior design accessible for every first-home buyer, with curated collections tailored to small urban spaces.

Mission

Deliver artisan furniture, smart lighting, and zero-emission delivery bundles backed by responsive services and community education.

Objectives

  • Launch a responsive website with at least seven interconnected pages.
  • Showcase logo, poster, and brand voice consistently.
  • Provide transparent product and service information.
  • Collect customer insights using a structured feedback form.

About the Idea

The EcoGlow team wants to promote small eco-friendly items. The store focuses on two things: light furniture made from bamboo or recycled wood, and simple smart lighting packs for beginners.

Vision

Give first-time renters easy options for setting up green homes.

Mission

Share practical tips, list affordable kits, and let visitors book our basic services online without any complicated steps.

Objectives

  • Explain the business in plain language.
  • Keep every page connected through the nav bar.
  • Show at least one table, one list, and one external link.
  • Collect feedback through an HTML form.
